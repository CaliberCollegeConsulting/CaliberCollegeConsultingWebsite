About Alex Munoz

Raising the standard for college admissions guidance.

Our Mission

Helping Students Tell Their Real Story

Alex Munoz College Consulting was founded on a simple belief: every student has a genuine, compelling story to tell — they just need the right guidance to tell it well.

We started this practice after years of watching talented, hardworking students get lost in a process that too often rewards polish over authenticity. Our goal is to flip that script, helping students present their true selves with clarity and confidence.

Today, we work with students across the country, from first-generation applicants to competitive Ivy League hopefuls, all with the same personalized, one-on-one approach.

Our Values

✓ Authenticity first. We help you sound like you, not like everyone else.

✓ Honest guidance. We tell you what you need to hear, not just what's easy.

✓ Equity-minded. Every student deserves a real shot at their goals.

✓ Whole-student focus. Admissions is one part of a bigger life.

Meet Your Consultant

One Consultant, Every Step of the Way

Alex combines real admissions office experience with hands-on support for every part of the process — no hand-offs between specialists.

Alex Munoz, Founder & Lead Consultant

Alex spent over 8 years working in Undergraduate Admission at Southern Methodist University, recruiting students from across the U.S. and around the world. During that time, Alex visited more than 200 high schools throughout Texas, New Mexico, Maryland, San Francisco, CA, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East, and read over 9,000 applications.

That firsthand experience on the other side of the desk shapes everything Alex does today — from building a personalized college list and coaching students through every essay, to helping plan coursework and activities starting as early as freshman year. You work directly with Alex from your first conversation through decision day.
